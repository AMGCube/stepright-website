Straight answers on spec, installation and budget.

01 How thick should hybrid flooring be? For most homes — renovation or new build — around 7.5mm is the balanced choice. +

Hybrid flooring comes in 5.5, 6.5, 7.5, 8, 9 and even 11mm. Each thickness came from a different period and was made for a different job — so a bigger number isn’t automatically a better floor.

For most family homes, whether you’re renovating or building new, around 7.5mm is the balanced choice: solid and stable underfoot, without adding height you then have to deal with at doorways, skirting and the joins to other rooms.

That’s why it’s the one thickness we stock.

02 How thick should the wear layer be? Thicker isn’t always better. For a family home, 0.5mm is enough. +

The wear layer is the clear top layer that protects the timber look underneath. You’ll see 0.3, 0.5, 0.7mm, and even 1mm or more.

Thicker isn’t automatically better. The right choice depends on what the room is used for and how hard it’s used — and you pay for every extra tenth of a millimetre.

Wear layerMade for
0.3mmLight use, low-traffic rooms
0.5mmFamily homes, including kids and pets
0.7mm and upCommercial spaces with constant foot traffic

For a home, 0.5mm is enough. Going thicker means paying for commercial-grade wear you won’t use.
